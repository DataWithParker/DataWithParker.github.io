Background

Today I build the tools that energy teams wish they already had: regression models that predict consumption, dashboards that surface anomalies, and forecasting pipelines that feed real procurement decisions.

This portfolio reflects that full stack — from sensor data to boardroom strategy, from Python notebooks to Power BI dashboards.
